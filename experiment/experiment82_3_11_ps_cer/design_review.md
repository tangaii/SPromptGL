# Experiment 82.3.11 PS-CER

## Source review

SPromptGL builds weighted patient graphs from modality similarities and supervision, applies graph convolution, and uses PEN to match text to local patches and embed that information in global context. The paper reports tenfold CV. It distinguishes original ABIDE hyperparameters from the ones frozen for DARG here. The original Python code groups four 9-wide patches per modality for PEN and uses a global pooling path. E74.4-G2 runs the historical PLNNoPEN, so those PEN tensors do not exist in this execution. PS-CER reads the four native 36-wide value projections before the first intermodal attention and the four 36-wide outputs of the last CAM feed-forward layer after intermodal attention. Their origin, shapes, equality of the contextual flattening with CAM144, and observational hooks are audited. They represent modality-level and cross-modally contextualized features. The experiment makes no anatomical claim about tokens or Gaussian coordinates. ## Prespecified objective and actions

The same small networks, parameter count, isolated initialization, optimizer, losses, range of training and checkpoint rules are used for Medical (G1) and Matched Non-semantic (G2). G1 uses the verified historical eight CLIP queries, TD rows 0–3 and ASD rows 4–7. G2 uses an independent seeded isotropic matrix, zero dependence on the embeddings except matching eight row norms. Query geometry consequently differs. Current CAM features are the only patient information provided to either group.

A learned cosine scorer maps native local and contextual tokens to 32 dimensions. Each uses log-mean-exp across four queries per class and a learned Null token in the same per-token softmax. Pool over four CAM tokens, then take the conditional ASD-minus-TD probability. Average the local and contextual signed differences, multiply by non-Null mass, (1-|s_l-s_g|/2), and the average of 0.5 and 0.5 plus half the text attention concentration. Match reliability is an internal score. The frozen text vectors have no gradient.

One FP64 Stiefel basis starts at historical U0. FP64 legal-train CAM class mean and second moment EMA (0.1) is projected onto the current basis. Complete regularized Gaussian class covariances have condition number bounded by 256. Coordinate terms from (h_c\odot\Sigma_c^{-1}h_c) plus equally distributed log determinants sum to the full Gaussian LLR. The ASD-minus-TD LLR is scaled by its legal-warmup 75th absolute percentile and passed through tanh. Spectral reliability uses average conditional-to-marginal variance ratio and the nearest-class Mahalanobis distance. No label or teacher enters action selection.

For disease sign (s_a\in\{+1,-1\}), form nonnegative support strengths (u = max(0, s_a × e_sem × r_sem − 0.015)) and (v = max(0, s_a × e_stat × r_stat − 0.04)). Disease prior (2(\sqrt{uv+10^{-8}}-10^{-4})\) has zero mass unless the deterministic off gap opposes the disease direction. The two candidates are signed, nonnegative direction-specific gains times the normalized projected off Jacobian; a small evidence-dependent network controls magnitude. Direction gains start exactly zero and are projected into [0,1] after AdamW. Each candidate is radially limited to 8% of its CAM norm. First-order gap movement has the requested sign. Actual nonlinear movement is measured and enters the router. Disease weights are the prior times exponentiated bounded learned scores, Keep uses its learned score with unit prior, and all learned scores start zero. Prior thresholds are in normalized evidence units, independent of test performance or head-confidence filtering. A normalized weight of zero excludes an unsupported action. The deterministic score path is shared by train and inference. Inference executes the best-weighted feature action, preferring Keep on ties. Hold-out hard actions are never conditioned on labels.

One untouched historical forward captures the mask and Keep logits. Two additional score computations use the original head with the same mask in place of rerunning its random operator. Let n(a) denote actual NLL under each feature action. The route loss is the legal class-macro mean of the sum of pi(a) × [n(a) − n(Keep)] over ASD and TD. We add this exact risk *replacement increment* to the native objective. It retains the native class loss and counts protected rows only through that historical component. On legal rows it replaces the balanced Keep component with exact expected risk. Preflight logs the original class-weight mass. The first zero-action step has mathematically zero shared risk increment derivative. We use the native shared derivative explicitly at this step and separately compute the new-parameter risk derivative, preserving bit-exact native updates at zero. There is no hard selection in the supervised classification loss, straight-through estimator, sampled candidate, or ensembling of opposing deltas. Argmax NLL, expected NLL, actual scores and masks are audited.

Additional legal-train objectives are:

- **Semantic** (.02): class-balanced local and contextual conditional NLL plus a .25-weighted Null Brier loss targeting lack of joint true-class support, with that target detached. This trains local and contextual matching rather than the router directly.
- **Statistical** (.02): class-balanced true-direction LLR logistic loss, softly bounded Bhattacharyya separation (weight .25), and .05 subspace offset and effective-rank lower guard (.9 of the origin projected effective rank). These optimize coherent disease statistics and avoid relying on rank or Jacobian coverage alone.
- **Safety** (.03): class-balanced expected squared negative same-mask true-class margin change, weighted by the detached reliable-correct Reference margin. It penalizes harming a previously reliable case only when a candidate makes its decision worse.
- **Residual** (.001): expected squared relative CAM action norm with an epsilon denominator. No constant activation reward or Keep target.
- **Function** (.20 with a separately capped actual gradient): class-balanced mean of KL(ref∥off) on matched true masks and with classifier Dropout removed, both legal-train only. Both paths use CAM tokens from the same historical-randomness upstream forwards. The teacher is an exact detached synchronous native Reference. Only shared parameters receive this gradient. The total applied addition is capped at 20% of each shared block's main gradient. The other terms inject only new-parameter gradients, with the combined weighted vector at most 25% of the main new-parameter norm. We retain .10's per-block reference-centered parameter and moment bounds and independently verify the actual Adam operation and projection.

The solver and regularizers operate at scales bounded near unity using tanh, dimension-normalized moments and stability guards. The weight caps are fixed to keep auxiliary steps subordinate across stages. Preflight checks magnitudes on legal training patients. No test result informs any of these values.

## Frozen decisions

Performance GO for G1 uses all user-specified unrounded paired thresholds, gain and patient net beyond Fold 2, and complete audit. Mechanism GO additionally needs at least one genuinely corrected B0 error still wrong with residual off, positive direct B net, credible nonzero interventions and same-epoch held-out function measurements. At the historical Best epoch, the paired residual-off versus Reference ACC decline must stay within 0.50 pp and mean absolute ASD probability change within 0.05 over covered folds. All ten folds must be covered for this condition. Function preservation on train, parameter preservation, and held-out same-epoch function are reported separately. Medical evidence requires G1–G2 positive paired ACC, nonnegative BA, positive paired patient net, more ACC wins than losses, positive ACC and net beyond Fold 2, and positive ACC after removing the largest contributing fold.

B0 is E74.4-G2 from its read-only archive. Formal G1 and G2 use the same single seed and historical folds. The historical selection using test ACC, preprocessing before CV, transductive patient graph and two overlapping identities limit independent generalization claims. The synchronous reference is a protection mechanism. It does not enter test inference.
