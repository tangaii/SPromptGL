# Experiment 45.2 — Task-Information-Faithful PID Estimation

This experiment asks which disease-related information in the historical ABIDE PHENO/ANAT/FUNC/CORR inputs is predictively detectable and how much is preserved by three preregistered discrete representations. It does not train SPromptGL, B0-B, GFR, PEN, CLIP, or an alignment model. The six pairwise estimates are two-source BROJA PID estimates; they are not a four-source PID.

The outer protocol is the historical `StratifiedKFold(10, shuffle=True, random_state=10)` with seed 0. Only each outer training fold is used. The input CSV is 871×320 and already reflects historical cohort-wide preprocessing and pre-CV RFE. No RFE-before source was located during initial inventory; all conclusions are restricted to this historical feature matrix and inherit its possible leakage.

The frozen methods are `A_UNSUP_KMEANS` (K=4, sensitivities 3/6), `B_TASK_AWARE_SCORE` (cross-fitted logistic disease score quantiles, K=4/6), and `C_TASK_STRUCTURE_HYBRID` (two OOF disease-score states crossed with 2/3 unsupervised structural states, total support 4/6). B is a diagnostic for redundancy/unique disease effects and is not accepted alone as a synergy estimator. C is a candidate, not presumed correct. Quantile boundaries are label-independent empirical quantiles. All supervised score encoders used for an evaluated sample are fit without that sample's label.

The prediction quantity `J_OOF = H(Y)-CE_OOF` is reported in bits as an OOF predictive-information proxy. It is signed and is not called mutual information. Predictive gains are not equated with PID synergy. Bootstrap intervals for frozen codes and pipeline-refit sensitivity are kept separate. Full-pipeline permutation retrains every label-dependent score encoder under the permuted labels.

Primary sources for the theory/protocol audit: [To Align or Not to Align, AAAI 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39248); [Quantifying & Modeling Multimodal Interactions, NeurIPS 2023](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/575286a73f238b6516ce0467d67eadb2-Abstract-Conference.html); [the PID authors' code](https://github.com/pliang279/PID); [Bertschinger et al., BROJA](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.2852); [SPromptGL, MICCAI 2025](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/0861-Paper5129.html). Local caches and source availability are recorded in `manifest.json`.

The frozen stages were run in this order: `python -B run.py prepare`, `preflight`, `synthetic`, `predict`, and `pid`. Run `python -B run.py report`, then `python -B run.py audit`; `python -B run.py handoff` appends to the project handoff only when the independent audit passes and the original handoff prefix still matches. Runtime caches and resampling traces are in `.runtime/`.
