# Experiment 59 Design Freeze

## Question and base

SHAPE-PEN tests whether training-fold, classifier-derived CAM-position evidence can control disease-level semantic supervision while the original 144-dimensional patient representation remains on the full classification path. The sole training base is Experiment 53.3 group `CADR_WD_0p01_GFR_WD_0p11`; its ten audited metrics and checkpoints remain read-only. Every new fold reconstructs GFR, CAM and CADR from the Experiment 43.1 untrained anchor.

The four raw input blocks are PHENO48, ANAT6, FUNC10 and CORR256. The repaired Experiment 51 GFR returns 320-dimensional fused patient features. The unchanged `PLNNoPEN` CAM produces four 36-dimensional position vectors, flattened to 144 dimensions for the original `OutputLayer`; that layer then maps to a 36-dimensional hidden state and the two-class classifier. `attn_embedding` is accepted but unused by `OutputLayer.forward`. Experiment 51's four-way GFR aggregation is retained. Experiment 53.3's reliable CADR disease propagation and original-graph fallback are retained, as are its GFR, CAM, classification, graph, pair, adversarial and relation-preservation losses.

## Paper and implementation audit

SPromptGL's original PEN groups eight frozen CLIP descriptions into two broad classes, applies an MLP to the grouped text features, then uses maximum text similarity as a multiplicative weight on CAM's four-by-four-by-nine position tensor before the OutputLayer. This is not a disease-prototype contrastive objective. Experiment 59 uses the PEN-free CAM selected by Experiment 53.3 and adds disease-level objectives to the common 144-dimensional representation.

BrainPrompt's official ABIDE label file contains two shared descriptions in `[TC, ASD]` order, and its official `abide_label.pt` stores two 2048-dimensional frozen Llama-encoder embeddings in that same order. The released graph-model code applies one shared `Linear(2048, out_dim)` to both vectors, normalizes patient and label representations, uses their cosine similarities as unscaled logits, and adds ordinary cross-entropy over those logits with `lambda1` defaulting to `0.001`. Experiment 59 reorders the cached embeddings to `[ASD, TD]`, applies the same shared-linear/cosine-CE mechanism at 144 dimensions, and calibrates the coefficient using training-fold gradient scale; this is reported as BrainPrompt-style disease supervision, not a full BrainPrompt reproduction. It does not import BrainPrompt's GNN, ROI graph or metadata branches.

SABER's paper defines a Graph-CLS query, patient-text cross-attention and gated residual injection in equations (8)-(10), followed by classification CE and a temperature-scaled disease-prototype contrastive term in equations (11)-(13). The paper reports that its SABERlite ABIDE accuracy is 69.82% and full SABER accuracy is 71.58%, on its own data and protocol; those values are not directly comparable with the 53.3 historical reference. SABER has no public implementation identified in its paper page, so only equation (12)'s disease loss is reproduced with the same BrainPrompt disease anchors and current 144-dimensional representation. Its Graph-CLS, cross-attention, residual injection and hypergraphs are not imported. Temperature and coefficient are training-fold gradient-scale adaptations, not claimed paper values.

SHAPE-PEN differs from both losses by deriving a per-patient, stop-gradient reliability from four deterministic CAM-position substitutions. It decomposes the projected shared representation into one prototype-difference direction and an orthogonal residual; only the one-dimensional disease component is semantically supervised, while the untouched full 144-dimensional vector remains the sole classifier input. SHAPE scores this component by its dot product with normalized prototypes: cosine normalization would cancel the magnitude of a one-dimensional projection and yield a near-zero patient-representation gradient. The evidence weight is bounded below and depends on positive support, conflicting evidence and total margin sensitivity. It is not a learned attention gate or an edge-level causal claim.

## Frozen group contrasts

All groups use the same 144-dimensional patient representation, fixed splits, seed, warmup, original losses, original classifier, checkpoint rule and optimizer streams. G01 versus G02 contrasts unscaled direct cosine CE with temperature-scaled disease contrastive CE. G03 controls G02 with frozen anonymous vectors matched to the official text pair's norm and cosine. G04 replaces the fixed language vectors with two learnable numerical vectors. G05 uses only outer-train labels in standard supervised contrastive learning. G06 fixes rho to one while retaining partial alignment. G07 uses patient rho without the partial subspace. G08 adds rho-weighted original classifier CE while retaining the full original classification loss. G09 permutes rho only among same-class training patients. G10 changes only SHAPE-FULL's fixed disease text bank to geometry-matched anonymous vectors. G11 is SHAPE-FULL.

The shared text projection has the same initialization across all groups within each fold and is initialized inside an isolated RNG scope. The anonymous bank matches the official pair's per-vector norm and inter-vector cosine before projection. G04 adds two learnable 2048-dimensional prototypes (4,096 parameters); all projection parameters are shared in shape across the text-alignment groups. New objectives use a separate AdamW optimizer at 0.001 with weight decay 0.001; GFR/CAM Adam and CADR AdamW settings are unchanged. Loss coefficients are frozen by one outer-train-only preflight that targets a 0.10 semantic-to-original-classification gradient norm ratio at the CAM representation; it does not inspect validation/test performance.

## Evidence and RNG controls

The primary classifier forward remains one unchanged stochastic CAM/OutputLayer call per training step. Its CAM output is captured by a shadow forward method verified bitwise against `PLNNoPEN.forward`. Four substitutions reuse that same CAM tensor and use a deterministic functionally equivalent OutputLayer scoring path with dropout disabled. This path runs under `no_grad`, uses no GFR/CAM re-encoding, and is checked not to change CPU or CUDA RNG state. Training evidence references use leave-one-out means computed only from outer-train CAM positions; test-only descriptive evidence uses the outer-train mean and the model's predicted class. Test labels never enter rho or any training loss.

Rho is `rho_min + (1-rho_min) * positive_support_fraction * tanh(total_absolute_margin_change / fold_scale)`, where `rho_min=0.2` and the fold scale is the outer-train 75th percentile with a fixed 0.1 lower bound. A class-balanced weighted mean gives ASD and TD equal loss contribution. Rho is detached. When all position changes are zero, rho equals its floor.

## Experiment 58 failure not repeated

Experiment 58 FAVE-PEN was audited NO-GO: it had only one directionally supported connection among 252 named selected edges, its text group did not beat the matched numerical group, its residual barely changed CORR positions, and fixed-checkpoint interventions produced no hard-label flips. Experiment 59 does not resume its anatomical hypothesis matching, does not change CAM positions at inference, and makes no claims about specific ROIs or causal modality contributions. CAM-position substitution deltas are model-functional evidence after cross-modal interaction, not independent modality ablations.

## Evaluation limits

The historical ten-fold split, CV-before-fold preprocessing, 871-node transductive graph and `val=test` checkpoint selection are kept for exact protocol matching. This is a single-seed exploratory comparison, not an unbiased external-generalization evaluation. Text-loss response, orthogonal projection geometry and classifier sensitivity do not establish biomedical validity or a biological disease/personal-information disentanglement.

## Pre-registered descriptive decision gates

The frozen configuration defines GO as at least +0.5 percentage points mean ACC over the 53.3 reference, six or more ACC-winning folds, and no more than a 1-point mean SEN-ASD loss. It also requires positive mean ACC and at least six wins against BrainPrompt-style, SABER-style, learnable-prototype and SupCon controls; G11 must beat G10 by at least 0.5 ACC points with six wins to claim independent language value. Mechanism gates require G11 to beat the uniform, global-gated, weighted-CE and shuffled-evidence controls in at least six ACC folds. Evidence non-collapse requires training-rho SD above 0.02, less than 80% at either boundary, and an absolute ASD/TD mean-rho gap below 0.2. Neighborhood retention and effective rank are reported as descriptive mechanism evidence. These thresholds do not make ten folds independent clinical samples or establish statistical significance.
