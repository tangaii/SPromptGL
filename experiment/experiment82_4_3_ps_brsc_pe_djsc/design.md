# Experiment 82.4.3 — Prompt-guided Reliable Spectral Correction

## Question and provenance

The experiment tests whether preserving the native prediction path and conditioning bounded CAM144 corrections on patient-level correctability can improve ASD/TD decisions. PS-BRSC uses the E82.2-G2 statistical and semantic spectrum weighting to select directions and learns independently signed actions. PE-DJSC uses the E82.3.2-G2 Gaussian disease evidence estimator and constrains the sign of spectral and complementary Jacobian displacements to the evidence.

The SPromptGL paper describes CAM followed by PEN, where SPGS selects local responses to disease text and RES integrates local details with global features. The author's repository implements PEN in PLN. The user repository caches the original RN50 text encoding, and the five core source files inspected on the server match the user repository revision in config.json. The actual historical E74.4-G2 path uses the vendored PLNNoPEN and Positive-DCR implementation. The path for both new groups is historical multimodal data → GFR → DARG Positive-DCR → PLNNoPEN CAM → CAM144 → additive correction → original OutputLayer. The four input modalities remain part of DARG. Spectral coordinates are constructed from the full CAM144 vector. The output is processed by the original LayerNorm(144), Linear(144,36), GELU, Dropout(0.35), Linear(36,2) and log-softmax.

[E82.4.2](../experiment82_4_2_sacs_pdjc/results/report.md) had a direct net correction of +1 in each group and concurrent native degradation of −5 and −6 patients. This protocol tests native supervision alongside the two new correction mechanisms. These simultaneous design changes limit separate causal attribution for differences between experiments.

The eight exact archived RN50 queries and matched anonymous queries preserve order and spelling. Both groups use 16 spectral tokens and one learned Null for cosine attention. The native objective adds 0.10 times cross-entropy of the current original no-Dropout head applied to the same legal CAM features already captured for the full pass. Original head weights are differentiable. Original full-path classification, graph, pair, adversarial and preservation losses use the untouched historical implementation. The residual-only CAM gradient is 0.25 of its ordinary magnitude, following the immediately preceding spectral experiments. The direct CAM gradient and native auxiliary path retain unit gain. Native added gradients are limited to 10% of the full historical classification gradient norm separately in GFR, DARG, CAM and head. Only the new prompt and control families receive the correction auxiliary gradients. Their separate caps are 5% alignment, 5% actual margin gain, 3% protection and 2% regularization of full classification. Applied gradients are measured after float32 addition. Zero main gradient implies zero added gradient. A separate AdamW(0.0005, 0.01) steps after the three historical optimizers.

Use m = logits_ASD − logits_TD. True margin is (1 − 2y)m. Both groups use class-balanced smooth gain targeting a positive fraction of first-order capacity on feasible, likely wrong training rows. The protection term uses the same current weights to penalize negative gain around the correct-side boundary. Weights for these objectives come from the unchanged deterministic off prediction and are fixed within the backward pass. An action-alignment loss in G1 uses the training true-class local direction even when the gate is small. In G2 the alignment loss uses plausible, reliable continuous evidence and the actual signed Jacobian displacement. G2 also penalizes squared relative residual more when evidence is weak. L_native uses ordinary CE on legal training labels.

## G1 · PS-BRSC

For C use z = (C − μ)U and whitened z for the token descriptors. E82.2-style direction and descriptor embeddings encode 16 keys. Coverage is one minus the product of one minus each of the eight attention masses for a direction, with the softmax over 17 keys. Normalize coverage to w_sem. Normalize its entropy by log(16). The spectrum MLP yields w_sp from absolute whitened coordinate, eigenvalue and disease covariance descriptors. A Null/entropy-dependent β mixes w_sem and w_sp. The token/context controller predicts signed a ∈ [−1,1] for each axis independently of its nonnegative importance. The controller also sees whitened coordinate, current margin and normalized spectral Jacobian coefficients.

Define J as the first derivative of current deterministic head margin at C, and J_U = (JU)Uᵀ for row vectors. Capacity B = 0.08‖C‖‖J_U‖ is a local first-order estimate. Feasibility is sigmoid((B − |m|)/(0.25B + 0.05)). A learned gate using weighted tokens, Null-dependent semantics, spectral scale, m, p(ASD) and B multiplies feasibility. The pre-limit residual is 0.12‖C‖ gate · ((w/‖w‖) ⊙ a)Uᵀ. Its final direction stays in U16. The nonzero small controller initialization supplies classification gradients in the first step. Fixed-weight interventions remove feasibility or remove action signs while holding the remaining learned outputs fixed.

## G2 · PE-DJSC

E82.3.2 standardized Gaussian moments are reproduced on legal E61 rows. The source E82.3.2 implementation used a single outer-training reference. This experiment applies that estimator to five archived stratified inner banks from E82.4.2-G2 and an all-legal inference bank. Only bank membership is inherited from E82.4.2-G2. Each legal training row uses a bank that excluded its own inner partition. Means and uncentered second moments have float64 EMA rate 0.10. Sample variance uses n/(n−1), then 90% class variance plus 10% count-weighted pooled variance and its historical floor. For whitened z, Gaussian logp(ASD) − logp(TD) is bounded with 8 tanh(LLR/8). Separation in Gaussian mean and variance relative to count-adjusted uncertainty, continuous in-distribution support, and a fixed class-balanced out-of-fold directional calibration form reliability. The calibration is rescaled to [0,1] from balanced signed tanh(LLR/2) on legal E61 rows.

Normalize continuous matched Normal/Abnormal Prompt coverage, blended by per-coordinate evidence sign preference, to w. Evidence is E = Σ w·reliability·bounded LLR, a reliability-weighted average across correlated axes. Positive E supports ASD, and negative E supports TD. Reliability enters both this evidence and patient-level gate context. Use d = tanh(E/0.30), conflict = sigmoid(−m d / 0.35), feasibility as in G1 with ‖J‖, and a learned Prompt- and evidence-conditioned gate times feasibility, conflict and a reliability factor. The pre-limit dual-space displacement is 0.12‖C‖ gate d times the sum of normalized J_U and J−J_U with separate sigmoid strengths. Both positive strengths are in (0,1). Jacobian carriers are detached. The margin path retains its first-order CAM derivative. Semantic-off replaces attention with uniform mass and eliminates Query context. Evidence-direction-off retains |d| and isolates the signed direction. Reliability-off replaces per-axis reliability with one. Spectral-only and Complement-only mask the alternate raw component, then apply the same bound.

Train with prior EMA, step the four optimizers, evaluate once using the prior EMA, select Best, then update EMA from the captured legal eval-mode CAM. Best preserves the prediction-time EMA. Latest preserves the updated EMA and the evaluation-time snapshot needed to reproduce its saved prediction. The five inner banks only serve statistical estimation.
