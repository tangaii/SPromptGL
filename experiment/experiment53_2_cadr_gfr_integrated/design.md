## Source and paper audit

- SPromptGL MICCAI 2025 Section 2.1 describes modality-specific patient relations, graph convolution, and complementary feature representation. The checked local implementation uses projected cosine similarity and a detached `score > 0.9` support mask; `GraphConv` computes `A_norm @ (X @ W)`, and each GCN has two parameterized layers with ReLU after layer one. `normalize_adj(A + I)` is symmetric degree normalization. Its public GFR has a PHENO overwrite at the ANAT branch; Experiment 51 `LegacyFixedGFR` repairs this by summing all four full-320D messages. The executed simple-2 objective is classification plus the mean `GraphConstructLoss`; the paper's label-derived adjacency term is not the optimized simple-2 loss. The original CAM is retained and PEN is physically absent.
- DeepASD first adversarially aligns modality feature embeddings, then constructs modality-specific patient similarity graphs, fuses them into a global graph, and classifies with a graph neural network. CADR-GFR borrows the motivation to align before graph use, while its relation features and CORR-anchored asymmetric adversary are different; it does not transplant DeepASD's encoder, global graph, or classifier.
- MGDR motivates preserving shared and modality-private evidence instead of assuming shared representation is sufficient. MM-GTUNets emphasizes dynamic modality-specific graph construction and cross-modal interaction. SMGD distinguishes shared/complementary diagnostic evidence from incidental agreement and non-robust modality correlations. AAAI 2026 “To Align or Not to Align” reports that enforced alignment can help or hurt depending on cross-modal redundancy. These motivate reliability-gated, asymmetric alignment; they do not validate CADR-GFR.
- Historical evidence reviewed: Experiment 43.1 verified the repaired four-way GFR and anchor/split provenance. Experiment 47.1/52 report strong weight decay shrinks learned GCN matrices/messages; Experiment 47.3 found general propagation can matter while true patient identity did not beat strong rewiring controls. Experiment 48.2 reports relation AUC near chance and no disease-relation gain. Experiment 53.1 reports CORR-only patient-disjoint OOF reliability (historical mean AUC 0.7257; other modalities near 0.5), executable but not beneficial adversarial effects, and small DRAP propagation effects without a diagnostic gain. These are read-only prior findings, not this run's results. Experiment 51's checked `LegacyFixedGFR`, `PLNNoPEN`, training code, optimizers, and configuration are the sole engineering base. Experiment 51 per-fold checkpoints are not present in the `/data` copy; its supplied aggregate and Experiment 53.1 read-only fold references will be used only for comparison.

## Frozen CADR-GFR

There is one formal group, `E1_CADR_GFR_FULL`, ten folds, seed 0. The only classifier input is one 871-by-320 GFR result sent to the unchanged Experiment 51 CAM. No standalone DRAP residual or extra classifier is used.

Each modality has an independent 32-D `Linear → GELU → LayerNorm` relation encoder, a symmetric 65-D pair feature `[abs(z_i-z_j), z_i*z_j, cosine(z_i,z_j)]`, and a `65 → 32 → 1` relation scorer. `same_diagnosis_pair` supervision uses only outer-train patient pairs. Three patient-disjoint inner folds estimate per-modality OOF AUC/PR-AUC and fit a positive-slope Platt calibration from OOF logits. OOF reliability is valid only when AUC ≥ 0.55 and the same-diagnosis score mean exceeds the different-diagnosis score mean; `r_m = clamp((AUC_m - 0.5)/0.25, 0, 1)` for valid modalities, otherwise zero. This rule is recomputed per fold.

The four relation representations enter a four-way modality discriminator with a two-value same/different condition. CORR is the stopped-gradient anchor in the adversarial branch; the three student modalities receive GRL scale 0.03. All four modalities retain BCE pair supervision. The relation-preservation term is `mean(relu(0.02 - (mean(logit_same)-mean(logit_different))))`, weighted by 0.01. The 0.02 logit margin was frozen after an outer-train-only initialization scale check: random-init relation-logit SD was 0.0098–0.0248 across modalities. Pair and adversarial coefficients are 0.05 and 0.01.

Each original GraphLearn graph remains the source of the threshold-0.9 candidate support and the unchanged graph loss. A deterministic mutual top-12 cap is applied only to the disease-graph candidate support; the fallback to fixed modality-feature cosine top-12 is enabled per fold only when the anchor candidate graph has fewer than one undirected candidate edge per patient. The four disease graphs are independently scored and built. For calibrated `q_m`, positive edges require `q_m ≥ 0.55`, negative edges `q_m ≤ 0.45`, and uncertain edges are skipped. Disease edge weights are original similarity × signed confidence × OOF reliability × sigmoid(edge gate), with symmetric support. Positive and negative adjacency matrices are normalized separately and never combined into a signed degree matrix.

For each modality, the same anchor-initialized two-layer 320-D GCN computes `H_pos = GCN(X, norm(A_pos + I))` and `H_neg = GCN(X, norm(A_neg + I)) - GCN(X, I)`. Its internal bounded neighbor-difference channel uses `sum_j(w_ij*(X_j-X_i))/max(1,sum_j w_ij)`, followed by modality-block RMS-tanh bounding. The message is `M_m = H_pos - 0.25*H_neg + 0.02*D_m`. Final output is `H = X + sum_m sigmoid(fusion_weight_m)*alpha_m*M_m`, with trainable `alpha_m` initialized from fold-local OOF reliability and bounded by sigmoid. If CADR is disabled, the model calls the exact repaired Experiment 51 E1 path.

The objective is `L_cls + L_graph + 0.05*L_pair + 0.01*L_adv + 0.01*L_preserve`. Original GraphLearn/GCN/fusion parameters use Adam at lr 0.0038, WD 0.11; CAM uses the same Adam settings. Added relation, discriminator, and gate parameters use AdamW lr 0.001, matrix WD 1e-4, bias/normalization WD 0. No test labels enter pair construction, calibration, adversarial conditions, edge selection, or the forward pass.

## Initialization, warmup, and evaluation

For each fold, seed `510000 + fold*100 + 1`, construct the Experiment 51 repaired GFR, then the original CAM, load all shared tensors from the corresponding Experiment 43.1 untrained anchor, and capture the post-construction RNG. New CADR modules are initialized inside `torch.random.fork_rng` with fold-local seed `532000 + fold`, restoring Python/NumPy/Torch CPU/CUDA states afterwards. Pair sampling, inner folds, and calibration use local deterministic RNGs. The anchor's serialized `rng` is not blindly restored: the Experiment 51 E1 contract is the rederived post-construction state, which matches the Experiment 53.1 initialization audit; per-fold hashes are recorded.

Epochs 0–9 train only the new relation/adversarial modules with pair losses while classification uses the exact E1 forward and only E1 losses update shared GFR/CAM parameters. Epoch 10 onward enables CADR-GFR and formal best-checkpoint selection. Max 450 epochs, strict ACC improvement, `wait > 200`, and historical `val=test` are unchanged. Every best checkpoint is independently reloaded and reevaluated. The report will identify the shared-patient, CV-preprocessed, transductive and `val=test` biases; this single seed is exploratory, not an unbiased generalization estimate.
