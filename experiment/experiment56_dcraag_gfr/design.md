# Experiment 56 — DCRAG-GFR

## Frozen question and status

The single formal group is `E56_FULL_DCRAG_GFR`, seed 0, ten fixed E51 folds. It tests whether four disease-conditioned, adversarially aligned patient-relation branches can replace E51 E1's GFR, reweight four separate graph supports, and propagate useful same- and different-disease messages into the unchanged E1 CAM. No extra formal training group or parameter search is allowed. All results are historical-protocol exploration, not unbiased independent testing.

## Source and paper map

The MICCAI 2025 SPromptGL paper's Figure 1 describes modality relation graphs, graph feature refinement (GFR), and CAM fusion. Section 2.1/Eq. (1) describes graph smoothness and degree terms; Eq. (2) gives the DGCN update `F^(l+1)=ReLU(A F^l Theta)`. The paper's Eq. (7) combines graph and label objectives. The reported ablation and ABIDE table compare CAM, GFR and later components. The paper is the method claim, not the executable specification.

In the audited source, `GraphLearn.forward` projects one modality, L2-normalizes rows, computes a dense cosine matrix, and hard-masks scores at threshold 0.9. The four GraphLearn modules each construct one graph. All four GCNs take the concatenated 320-dimensional PHENO/ANAT/FUNC/CORR tensor. The historical aggregation code assigns the first message, overwrites it at the second branch, and accumulates later branches; E51 E1's `LegacyFixedGFR` fixes this by starting at raw X and adding all four sigmoid-weighted messages. The E1 path computes the mean of four `GraphConstructLoss` values, while its `simple-2` total optimizes only weighted classification NLL plus that graph loss; the separately computed ALoss is not included. CAM is `PLNNoPEN`: the PEN parameter path has been physically removed, and CAM receives the 320-D GFR output. E1 has a GFR Adam and CAM Adam, both LR 0.0038 and WD 0.11.

E51 source used for the private snapshot: `run.py` SHA256 `32fc05c0e8c1211947f3003eae74d705eb4087e629b8a6af0fdf1878299a8718`; `dugsa.py` `ee59de1eb00fad670a90c732356e2798c31481b9199701ed4bccc6c6c9a76f12`; `config.json` `f3447e150bc67ab29860dc0ae4804c194d5607fcbbdeccc9066ae1600c6ba531`; `network.py` `5542f52d598e2f4d32aaad0c46b80c214fd562f74110a1d0ffcc619b9aefb3d1`; `layers.py` `4f7c07861bdeeb35fe156448aac4b32bae1b2f813c957d679f50dbd0225e819a`; `utils.py` `95a8cd0326bc95e90ff4e0a5fc85ebc2b4fd2150955bc975a604374d81267b67`. E51's effective E1 path is `build_state("E1", fold)`: seed `510000 + 100*fold + 1`; instantiate the four GraphLearn and four full-320 GCN branches; load the fold's audited GFR anchor; instantiate CAM in historical order, apply the historical linear initializer and load the fold anchor; then create the original optimizer state. The best E1 checkpoints are not initial weights. The E43.1 fold anchor is the verified initialization source used by E51 E1. The anchor's historical `parameter_sha` covers its original GFR+PLN parameter namespace; E56 additionally compares every inherited GFR and CAM tensor and records a separate wrapper-state SHA, so unlike-name digest values are not misreported as tensor mismatch. Experiment55 E5 reached 91.6144% ACC vs E51 E1 91.9592%, and its no-message model recovered the E1 baseline. These are historical results, not substitute measurements for E56.

## Frozen model

The four E1 GraphLearn and four E1 320-D GCN modules are transferred by object identity into one `DCRAGGFR`; the original GFR is not called after the new model and no E1-output residual adapter is used. A reference-only bypass preserves the exact E1 order for parity tests. The four raw modality views are 48/6/10/256 dimensions, and the 320-D concatenation remains each original GCN's input.

Each modality has its own pair encoder. Its symmetric input is `[abs(h_i-h_j), h_i*h_j, cosine(h_i,h_j)]`; a 32-D code splits into 16-D shared and 16-D private relation representations. A per-modality AA/TT/AT head predicts disease-pair relation. Train-patient labels supply relation targets only for pairs whose two endpoints are in the outer train fold. Pair supervision is balanced across AA/TT/AT and generated from a fold-local RNG. Shared, not private, codes enter a true gradient-reversal four-way modality-source discriminator conditioned by the frozen pair soft label. The source classes are PHENO/ANAT/FUNC/CORR. The discriminator minimizes source cross-entropy; GRL reverses only the shared-relation encoder gradient. No test label enters the teacher, relation labels, graph weights, or messages.

A CORR-only five-fold patient-disjoint logistic teacher creates train OOF probabilities and full-outer-train predictions for held-out patients. Pair conditions are the normalized AA/TT/AT products and are detached. Separate five-fold patient-disjoint pair classifiers estimate continuous modality reliability from outer-train patients only using the symmetric raw-pair basis `abs(x_i-x_j)`, `x_i*x_j`, and cosine. An initial three-scalar probe was rejected before formal training because it erased CORR's known relation signal; the high-dimensional symmetric basis is frozen across all folds. Reliability weights remain continuous and are floored only for numerical support; they do not hard-disable a modality. Cross-modal consistency is reliability-weighted and acts on shared relation probabilities, not private representations.

Each GraphLearn runs independently on its own raw modality to form its original dense adjacency and original graph loss. The PHENO mapping confirms 20 one-hot `SITE_ID_*` fields at offsets 26–45. Per-modality candidate support is the bounded union of the original hard graph, top-16 cosine neighbors and top relation-teacher candidates; learned relation scores reweight only within that support, and support construction does not use labels. Nonnegative normalized AA+TT and AT channels remain separate. Four original GCNs propagate on the same-disease channel; four low-rank difference maps propagate `X_i-X_j` over AT edges. A patient-conditioned gate mixes four messages, initialized uniformly and scaled by the inherited sigmoid fusion weights. A bounded, patient-specific, initially unit propagation scale produces the final `X + beta_i * sum_m omega_im M_im` 320-D CAM input. This is an internal unified GFR computation, not an external residual on E1 output.

The frozen total objective is weighted E1 classification NLL + original mean graph regularizer + `0.02 L_relation + 0.003 L_adversarial + 0.002 L_consistency + 0.001 L_edge_stability`. GRL maximum is 0.03 with a 20-epoch warm-up. GraphLearn and CAM keep E1 Adam LR/WD (0.0038/0.11); inherited GCN weights use LR 0.0038 and fixed WD 0.0001 to address the historically observed message shrinkage; new modules use AdamW LR 0.001 and WD 0.0001. This GCN optimizer change is an intentional method change and E56 is not optimizer-identical to E1.

## Protocol, audit and decision

Data must match 871 patients, 403 ASD label 0, 468 TD label 1, 320 ordered columns and the frozen CSV/mapping hashes recorded in `manifest.json`. Folds are `StratifiedKFold(10, shuffle=True, random_state=10)`. The historical 450-epoch cap, `wait > 200`, strict ACC improvement, val=test checkpoint selection, preprocessing/RFE before outer CV, and 871-node transductive graph are retained. Hard-AUC is computed from hard predictions; probability AUC uses continuous ASD probability with ASD positive. Fold AUCs and pooled OOF probability AUC are distinguished.

Before formal training, tests must check inherited tensors/buffers and hashes, isolated new initialization, entry RNG, exact E1 bypass and CAM parity, soft-condition sums/symmetry, label isolation, GRL sign, gradients to all four edge/message paths, finite bounded propagation, and save/restore equivalence. The ten best checkpoints are independently reloaded for prediction and metric reproduction. Fixed-checkpoint interventions are diagnostics only, not independent retraining evidence. `COMPLETE_AUDITED` requires all ten folds, readback parity, and unchanged protected hashes; otherwise the status is PARTIAL or FAILED.

Known limitations remain: `val_idx=test_idx` participates in checkpoint selection, global preprocessing/RFE precedes outer CV, the 871-node graph is transductive, only one seed and one ABIDE A cohort are used, and ten folds overlap in training subjects. No significance or external generalization claim is permitted.
