# Experiment 78.8 — FAPC design and audit record

## Frozen sources

The only trainable base is the SHA-checked E74.4-G2 Positive-DCR runner and its E61 epoch-10 warmup. The implementation reuses the read-only loader used by E78.7, preserving the three historical optimizers, graph/CAM forward order, test-fold ACC checkpoint selection, transductive graph, pre-CV preprocessing/RFE, and Fold-2 overlap handling. The new experiment does not import an E78.7 checkpoint.

SPromptGL is used for the GFR/CAM context and the original eight prompt strings. Its paper describes semantic prompt-guided local representation and relation-aware refinement; the original SPGS `max(softmax)` direction loss is not restored here. BrainPrompt contributes the two official ABIDE disease-level sentences, with TC mapped to TD=1 and ASD=0; its Llama encoder is not used. KgCoOp motivates a frozen hand-crafted semantic reference, while PCGrad motivates the one-sided conflict projection applied only to the auxiliary gradient entering CAM144. These are design inspirations, not source-code copies. See the primary sources: SPromptGL paper [MICCAI 2025](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/paper/5129_paper.pdf), BrainPrompt paper [MICCAI 2025](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/paper/2260_paper.pdf), KgCoOp [arXiv:2303.13283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13283), and PCGrad [NeurIPS 2020](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/3fe78a8acf5fda99de95303940a2420c-Abstract.html).

## FAPC definition

G1 uses the live CAM144 patient projection and two signed disease directions from legal training support patients. G2/G3 use ten frozen RN50 text vectors in one common CPU-float64 row-space SVD coordinate system. The 20-vector Gram matrix is retained to floating-point error and zero-padded to 32 dimensions; no trainable text projection exists. A shared patient-derived disease direction calibrates each prompt with `q=normalize(anchor+0.2*sigmoid(beta_raw)*v_c)`. The auxiliary signed semantic logits use five-prompt normalized log-mean-exp at temperature 0.25 and scale 10. Final prediction remains the original DARG `out["logp"]`; the semantic branch is auxiliary.

The objective is `L_DARG + 0.1 L_aux + 0.01 L_keep`, with `L_keep=mean(1-cos(q,anchor))` only for G2/G3. The support set is unique `(train-test)` and does not use LOO. Fold 2 rows 377 and 407 (zero-based 376,406) are excluded from new support/supervision while the historical base path remains unchanged.

For CAM144, `g_base=grad(L_DARG,h)` and `g_aux=grad(0.1 L_aux+0.01 L_keep,h)` are computed on the single captured forward graph. If their dot product is negative, only the auxiliary vector is projected orthogonal to the base vector. The auxiliary vector is then capped at 20% of the base norm, and a hook passes `g_base+g_aux_safe` upstream. Direct DARG classifier gradients and new-module gradients remain untouched. The exact projection, cap, conflict fraction, and zero-base fallback are recorded per epoch.

## Text cache and corrected control

All twenty strings are encoded directly by the SHA-verified OpenAI CLIP RN50 checkpoint (`afeb0e10...b6762`) using the repository tokenizer. The common float64 SVD row space preserves the full 20-text Gram matrix before zero-padding to 32 dimensions. The original pilot's G3 cache mixed CLIP vectors from an E60 class-only bank with unrelated text strings, and its text list itself contained neurodevelopmental/brain terminology. That pilot is archived as superseded and is not used for the final medical-versus-anonymous inference. The corrected G3 uses five distinct alpha-category and five distinct beta-category neutral descriptions, with no disease, brain, anatomy, or connectivity vocabulary. Its exact strings and SHA are frozen in `config.json` and the rebuilt cache before a fresh 30-task run.

The first correction changed the text bank and common SVD coordinates. A subsequent independent source inspection found the hook returned only the safe auxiliary gradient, omitting the main gradient. Both previous 30-task runs are scientifically invalid and are excluded from efficacy comparisons. They are preserved under `runtime/superseded_initial_run/` and `runtime/superseded_missing_main_gradient/` for provenance only. The final corrected implementation returns `g_base + g_aux_safe`, and its preflight independently checks the entire original-parameter gradient against the unmodified DARG gradient plus the safe auxiliary CAM144 vector-Jacobian product. It also verifies that, with the hook active and zero auxiliary loss, all original gradients are bitwise identical to ordinary DARG. A fresh 30-task run is frozen only after these tests pass; no coefficients, optimizer settings, or test-driven hyperparameters are changed.

## Evidence limits

The protocol intentionally retains historical `val=test`, transductive all-patient graph, pre-CV preprocessing/RFE, strict test-ACC model selection, single seed, and no-LOO training support. Results are therefore method-alignment evidence, not unbiased clinical generalization. A G2-vs-G3 difference is the direct test of medical text content; G1-vs-G2 tests whether text adds beyond visual prototype supervision. Frozen-anchor participation or auxiliary loss improvement alone does not establish medical validity.
